# Design System

## Colours
- Background: slate-50 / slate-950
- Primary: teal-600
- Warning: amber-500/700
- Danger: red-600/700
- Text: slate-900/600/400

## Typography
- Geist Sans

## Patterns
- Glass cards with frosted panels
- Soft borders
- High legibility

## Motion
- 150-220ms tab/button transitions
- Reduced-motion support
- No flashing alerts
