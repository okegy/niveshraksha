# NiveshRaksha Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: Title
**NiveshRaksha**
*Pause. Verify. Protect.*
A privacy-first safety platform for retail investors.

## Slide 2: The Problem
- **Vulnerability:** First-time retail investors and regional users are targeted by sophisticated scams.
- **Tactics:** Scammers use guaranteed returns, urgency, and SEBI impersonation.
- **Gap:** Current platforms focus on trading and performance, lacking a safe space to verify claims without judgement.

## Slide 3: The Solution
**NiveshRaksha** provides three core tools:
1. **Message Analyzer:** Detects red flags (urgency, unrealistic guarantees, credential requests) using explainable, deterministic rules.
2. **Entity Verifier:** Checks advisor claims against official records.
3. **Behavioural Pause:** A 30-second checklist that interrupts the psychology of a scam.

## Slide 4: Key Differentiators
- **Privacy-First:** Local PII redaction. No raw data hoarding.
- **Deterministic Safety:** We use clear rules, avoiding opaque LLM "hallucinations" for critical risk flags.
- **No Investment Advice:** Strict guardrails. A "low risk" score doesn't mean "buy".

## Slide 5: Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Frontend:** Next.js, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui.
- **Backend:** FastAPI, Pydantic, Python.
- **Integration:** Mock SEBI integration ready for live APIs. 

## Slide 6: Hackathon Impact
- **Track:** Digital Fraud and Scam Resilience.
- **Impact:** Empowers users to independently verify information. Highly scalable and adaptable to regional languages (English/Tamil ready in design).
